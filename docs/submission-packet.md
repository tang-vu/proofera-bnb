## Project description

ProofEra is a risk-aware marketplace for autonomous DeFi agents on BNB Smart Chain. Users begin with
a financial job—Pancake concentrated liquidity, bounded grid trading, sustainable yield, or Venus
health-factor protection—and inspect category-specific sources, freshness, downside, costs and
unknowns before granting authority. Four public read-only analyzers are registered on BSC testnet as
ERC-8004 agent IDs 1825 through 1828. Registration is presented as identity publication, never as
performance proof.

The control loop fixes chain, target, selector, token, cap, expiry and revoke ownership before an
agent can act. A retained Altana testnet lifecycle joins successful grant, one scoped zero-amount PTA
approval, revoke, and two-provider final authority absence. ProofEra distinguishes pending, failed,
unknown and expired outcomes and never blind-retries ambiguous writes.

For PancakeSwap, exact-block analysis refuses a profit claim when fees, gas and slippage are missing.
A separate owner-executed testnet LP has finalized approval and mint receipts plus dual-provider
post-state. Its 9,104-second outcome observed unchanged price/liquidity and zero fees; therefore the
submission claims no realized benefit or autonomous-agent advantage. Three preregistered TermiX
pairs retain raw agent/manual outputs, time, cost and 100/100 bounded quality scores in both lanes.
Mixed timing directions and the internal, unauthenticated reviewer boundary remain explicit.

The public Proof Room binds claims and blockers to retained artifact paths and SHA-256 values. The
governing rule is simple: missing evidence stays missing.
