### PTA/WBNB TTY transport evidence boundary

The historical v1 attempt was bound to exact release commit `1a89ddae5e1f575b39dda6134d3ccedaddda0adf`, tree `b28a12f78861183ece5ff1c3fce36c0abf094239`, runtime-manifest SHA-256 `0xd5212c7563d5d3e3b10d461b5dedcc1552766262c1c87a7057b4dc2876ed0ce0`, and canonical policy digest `0x9357b560a91b1c41ef8ef84ea39001c206f50fc9839a55102f7916152160a189`; both exact-release audits were `GO`. The `8,670`-byte policy had `11,560` unpadded-base64url characters, making its complete single-line frame `11,713` characters. Windows Console truncated the line and policy admission rejected it fail-closed. The policy was not admitted and ACT-03 owner confirmation was not reached. At most, read-only recovery/journal inspection occurred; the attempt made no fresh RPC call, accessed no custody artifact, did not invoke DPAPI or unlock custody, and created no signature, new transaction hash, send, receipt, pool, or liquidity. After the source changed, those v1 values became historical evidence only, not a current ACT-03/SEC-01/SEC-02/CAKE-02 release authority.

The current transport requirement uses v3 challenge/frame domains over the strict nonce-bound ASCII `BEGIN`/ordered `CHUNK`/`END` state machine introduced in v2, with one absolute five-minute deadline. Every line binds its line index, exact chunk count, policy byte length, and lowercase raw policy-byte SHA-256; each chunk also binds its chunk index. Only exact LF or CRLF is accepted. The hard content-line cap is `4,096` bytes, while maximum valid worst-case construction is exactly `2,618` bytes and is conservatively tested at `<= 2,700`. Total transport is bounded to `102,400` bytes, policy to `1..65,536` bytes, and each non-final payload to exactly `2,304` canonical unpadded-base64url characters; the derived chunk count is at most `38`. The historical `11,560`-character policy becomes six chunks/eight total bounded lines but remains non-authorizing. A one-off local Windows Console observation motivated these conservative constants, but its harness and result are not retained and it is not reproducible repository evidence. Repository unit tests establish only the transport arithmetic and parser behavior. Retired v1/v2 domains, truncation, bad encoding/length/hash, duplicate/missing/reordered lines, control/blank input, and trailing or buffered data reject before policy admission, fresh RPC, custody access, or owner authority. Those unit tests do not prove an empty OS input queue or protection from preload/malicious same-user code. A committed recovery release/tree/manifest, exact audits, matching generation-2 policy, owner-v5 confirmation, and explorer-verifiable receipt remain separate requirements.

## Evidence update rule

A row moves to `verified` only when the linked test/receipt/output exists and has been checked. UI code, a screenshot of a disabled control, or a planned transaction is not onchain evidence.
